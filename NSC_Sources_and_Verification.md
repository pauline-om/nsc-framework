**Disagreement to be aware of.** OpenAI's report says the leaders responsible for incident response did not realize the significance of the message-board activity. The Princeton CITP authors read the public record as showing that alerts on May 26, June 27, and July 5 were seen, and that the evaluation was kept running or resumed (July 7). The public record is incomplete, and the METR and Redwood report states that it did not assess safeguard effectiveness, the extent of the compromise, or OpenAI's investigation. The authors of the CITP piece also note that the METR and Redwood work was facilitated by access and credits from OpenAI.

## Items to verify before relying on them

1. **EU Omnibus formal adoption.** Sources describe a provisional agreement with formal adoption expected before August 2, 2026. Final publication in the Official Journal was not confirmed. Check before citing the December 2, 2027 and August 2, 2028 dates as final.
2. **Details of ongoing litigation and investigations** may have changed since this compilation.
3. **Claims about other labs** (including reported third-party incidents involving Meta, and statements that many similar escapes may be unreported) come from secondary reporting and researcher statements. They are not independently established.
4. **Employment claims** reported in the CITP piece about researchers who raised alarms are the authors' assertion and are not repeated in the main documents.
